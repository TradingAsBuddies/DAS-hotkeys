# FL week backtest — 2026-09-21 to 2026-09-25, on the stocks selected daily

**Verdict: the Fashionably Late rule as traded lost money every day but Thursday, and lost the
week under every variant tried.** Base rule: −$3,824 (−51R at the SIM unit of $75) on 170
trades, 7.6% win rate, 154 stops. Ninety-six of those stops hit within one minute of entry.

Data: Massive.com flat files, `us_stocks_sip/minute_aggs_v1`, all sessions from 04:00 ET, 23
tickers × 5 days (`~/market_data/{T}/{T}_{date}_minute.csv`). Script: `backtest/backtest_fl_week.py`,
which mirrors `tools/fl_forward_test.py`: 9-EMA × 34-SMA sign change on completed 1-minute bars,
signal-bar volume > 1.5× the prior 20-bar average, no entry before 09:45, one position per ticker,
two round trips per ticker per day, stop 1.5 × ATR(14) at entry, entry at the next bar's open ±
0.15, $75 risk, max 300 shares, flat at 15:55.

**Universe caveat.** Only 09-24 (14 names after the MU ban) and 09-25 (21 names) have published
gameplan files this week. Monday to Wednesday use the union of the two (23 names) and are
labelled as such; they answer "how would the rule have done on these names", not "on the names
we picked that day".

## Fidelity check against Thursday's live run

The live driver on 09-24 filled 16 entries (13 closed in session, 3 open when the laptop was
unplugged). The backtest's Thursday has 24 entries; **12 of the 16 live entries are matched by a
simulated entry within three minutes, same symbol and side.** The simulator sees every bar while
the driver cycled every 45 seconds, so it catches a few more crosses, and it sized at $75 while
the live run sized at $250, which is why live lost −$1,958 on the day the simulation shows
+$506: the simulation's Thursday profit is three holds to the close (SPY +603, XOM +561, QQQ
+417) that the live run either stopped out of earlier (SPY at 12:00) or entered on a different
cross.

## What this says about Monday

1. **Do not run this rule again as-is.** Five days, four variants, one positive day carried by
   three trades.
2. **The only thing that made money was staying in a trend.** Candidates worth a backtest, in
   order: require the cross to hold for N bars before entry; take only the first cross of the
   session per name; exit on the opposite cross instead of a fixed ATR stop; trade only the
   names whose 5-minute trend agrees.
3. **Breadth filtering could not be tested here**: flat files carry no `$VOLD`. Friday's live
   log has it and can be joined to Friday's bars for one day's evidence.
4. **Publish a gameplan every day.** Three of five days had no list to test against.

**Every candidate loses more than the rule it was meant to fix, or loses less only by trading
less.** Waiting for confirmation makes it worse (the move is over by the time the cross has
held). Filtering by the 5-minute trend cuts the win rate to 3.5%. Removing the stop turns the
one-minute chop into full-size losses. The best absolute number, −$2,350, is "take only the
first cross", and it has no positive day.

Conclusion: **the 1-minute 9-EMA × 34-SMA cross with a volume filter has no edge on these names
this week under any entry or exit tested.** The six trades that made money in the base run were
not a signal the rule can isolate; they were the survivors of 170 coin flips.

## The 15-minute timeframe (2026-09-26, second pass)

The first 15-minute row above was wrong for two reasons: a 34-bar SMA on 15-minute bars needs
prior days as warm-up, and in regular-hours mode the last bar starts at 15:45, before the 15:55
gate, so positions still open at the close were silently dropped from the tally. Both fixed
(`--warmup-days`, `--rth-only`, end-of-series close). Same rule otherwise: 9/34 cross, volume
> 1.5× the 20-bar average, 1.5×ATR(14) stop on 15-minute bars, gate 09:45, flat at the close.

| Period | Universe | Trades | Win rate | P&L | R | Positive days |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **This week 09-21..25** | plans / union | 15 | 46.7% | **+563** | **+7.5** | 3/5 |
| Prior week 09-14..18 | same 23 names | 30 | 20.0% | −676 | −9.0 | 1/5 |
| Two weeks | | 45 | 28.9% | −113 | −1.5 | 4/10 |
| 1-minute base, prior week, same names | | 176 | 4.5% | −5,049 | −67.3 | 0/5 |

This week's 15 trades: 7 winners averaging +$169, 8 losers averaging −$77, a 2.2:1 payoff.
**Remove the single best trade (INTC long 09-24 09:30 → close, +$587) and the week is −$24.**

Neighbourhood this week: volume 1.25× / 2.0× → +5.5R / +10.5R; stop 1.0 / 2.0 / 2.5 ATR →
+13.7 / +4.7 / +6.5R; gate 10:00 / 10:30 → +6.8 / +8.5R; warm-up length irrelevant; ending at
12:00 → +0.8R; 10-minute bars −5.8R; 20-minute +4.5R; 30-minute −0.4R. Prior week under the
same neighbours: −7.6R to −12.4R, except gate 10:30 at +3.0R on 14 trades.

**Verdict on 15 minutes.** It stops the bleeding: over two weeks the 15-minute rule is roughly
flat where the 1-minute rule lost about 118R on the same names. It is not evidence of an edge:
45 trades, one of which decides the sign of the good week, and a negative out-of-sample week.
If it is traded Monday, it is traded as an experiment sized at 1R, with the expectation of
about one signal per name per week, not as a validated system.

## Reproduce

```bash
# data (Massive S3 credentials in ~/.claude/.env)
#   see the market-data skill; files land under ~/market_data/{TICKER}/
cd backtest
python3 backtest_fl_week.py --json results/fl-week-base.json
python3 backtest_fl_week.py --eod 12:00
python3 backtest_fl_week.py --stop-floor-pct 0.5
python3 backtest_fl_week.py --atr-bars 5
# 15-minute, regular hours, warm-up; out of sample on the prior week
python3 backtest_fl_week.py --bar-minutes 15 --warmup-days 5 --rth-only --json results/fl-week-15m.json
```
