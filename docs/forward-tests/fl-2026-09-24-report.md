## Why it lost

- **The cross alone is a coin flip on this tape.** Thursday was a rates-driven flush with
  negative breadth (NYSE net volume −276K at the close, decliners 2:1). The 1-minute 9/34
  cross fired 27 times across 14 names between 07:30 and 13:37 and the driver took the 15
  that passed the gate, volume, and cap. Both sides lost: 8 shorts, 7 longs.
- **Stops at 1.5 × 1-minute ATR are inside the noise.** Two trades stopped within 30 seconds
  of entry (XLE, QQQ). The backtest said the same thing: 100% of trades exit on a stop.
- **Ticker selection did not help.** The gameplan's own thesis names (SPY, INTC, XLE) all
  lost on the FL rule; the names that made money did so by being left open.

## Defects found and fixed (v0.2.1)

1. **Missed flatten.** The host slept from about 13:37 to 06:21; the loop had no notion of
   elapsed wall time, so the 15:55 gate was never seen. Fixed: the driver measures the gap
   between cycles and flattens immediately when it exceeds ten minutes.
2. **Unsigned position.** The montage's `.POS` reports a 300-share short as `300`. The close
   script chose its side from the sign, so covering XOM sold 300 more. Fixed: the driver reads
   the signed quantity from `%POS` Type (3 = short) and passes `$FL_SIDE` / `$FL_QTY`; the
   script refuses any other side.
3. **`Panic` refuses outside market hours** (found in the premarket chain test). Replaced by
   per-symbol limit closes.
4. **No report on abnormal exit.** Report writing moved to its own function.
5. **Risk unit was wrong.** $250 per trade is neither R. The R-CONFIG says SIM 1R = $75; the
   09-25 run uses `--risk 75`.

## What is still open

- Stop width. 1.5 × ATR(14) on 1-minute bars is the spec, and it is too tight for this tape.
  Candidates: ATR on 5-minute bars, or a minimum stop of 0.5% of price. Needs a backtest run,
  not a guess.
- The two FL definitions (34-SMA here, VWAP in `fl_monitor_spec.md`) still disagree.
- Requiring the cross bar to close beyond the reference line by some fraction of ATR would
  remove the 7-second and 24-second stops.
